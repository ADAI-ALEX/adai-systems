# Taste brief

Written 12 November 2026 from the 18 references in the taste vault (`npm run taste`, then http://localhost:4319/taste). All 18 were sourced by Claude. Alex has not added his own picks yet: when he does, they outrank everything here and this brief gets rewritten.

## What keeps recurring

1. **The work is the hero.** Ghostty opens on its terminal running. tldraw opens on a canvas you can draw on. Oxide puts a live shell in the corner. None of them lead with a slogan. ADAI has real things to show (Glimmer's training lab, JEB Depot's HUD, Lockout's screens), so the first screen should show or be one of them.
2. **One loud idea per site.** Rauno's yellow circle. Modal's glyph cube. Basement's room. Snellenberg's oversized name. Each site commits to a single device and lets everything else go quiet around it.
3. **Flat colour, in blocks.** Mistral's orange steps, Poolsuite's pink ground, Rauno's yellow. Colour arrives as solid areas with hard edges. No gradients doing the work.
4. **Two voices in type.** A big plain statement face, and mono for anything technical: dates, statuses, counts, commands. Oxide, Teenage Engineering and SF Compute all do this.
5. **Computing as texture.** Terminals, pixel art, dot matrix, desktop windows. It suits a studio whose newest work is a terminal lab and a pixel-art game.
6. **Density with a clear hierarchy.** Oh No, PostHog and SF Compute pack a lot in. Tiny navigation, big content.
7. **Attitude in the copy.** "We make cool shit that performs." Short, specific, a bit cheeky.

## Tendencies

| | Direction of travel |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Very large and plain for statements, set tight. Mono for metadata. No decorative italics. Scale does the work that ornament usually does. |
| **Colour** | Either near-black with one or two signal colours, or a flat saturated ground. High contrast. Colour means something (status, live, new). |
| **Density** | High, but ordered. Tables and lists over cards. |
| **Motion** | Purposeful and specific: a cursor that reacts, a terminal that types, things that can be dragged. Stepped or snappy easing over soft floaty fades. |
| **Layout** | Asymmetric. Edge-to-edge type. Sidebars and index lists. Full-bleed colour bands. |
| **Interaction** | Something to play with in the first screen. The visitor does something and the page answers. |

## What to avoid

- The Browser Company look: cream ground, small serif italic, spaced caps. It is the generated-site default now.
- Violet and pink-to-blue gradients, glass cards, cards inside cards.
- A label above every section heading.
- Centred hero, three feature cards, testimonial, call to action.
- Fade-up on every block as it scrolls in.
- Mood imagery that could belong to any company. Real screenshots or nothing.
- The old ADAI site's own look: off-white paper, Geist, hairlines, no accent. This is a rebuild, not a restyle.

## What this means for ADAI

- Glimmer's own palette (amber, periwinkle and green on near-black) is a ready-made dark direction that belongs to the studio.
- The lineage of fourteen projects is the studio's best story. It should be something a visitor can explore, not a list they scroll past.
- STEVIE's real, unedited, slightly wrong replies are the most human thing in the archive. Use them.
- The copy should sound like the Glimmer README: plain, measured, honest about limits.
